*Based on CMS reported membership count. Independent analysis of public CMS data. Not affiliated with or endorsed by CMS or any health plan; views are the author's own. Includes H-contracts only (regional PPOs and PACE are not counted). Source: CMS Monthly Enrollment by Contract/Plan/State/County, September of each year. Retiree and employer-group plans are excluded. CMS suppresses county enrollment of 10 or fewer, which is counted as 0. Plans are the H-contracts an organization has in the state. Products are plan benefit packages (PBPs) with a service area in the state. Avg members per product is members divided by products. County coverage is the share of the state's counties where the organization has a plan with reported enrollment (counties CMS suppresses at 10 or fewer members are included), out of the counties where any MA plan reports enrollment that year. Member reach is the share of the state's MA members living in those counties. Both measure footprint with reported members, a lower bound of the true service area; hover the column headings or filter labels for the definition. Plan basis: All plan types, or HMO/HMOPOS plans only. Organizations are CMS parent organizations with known lineages merged (Centene with Health Net and WellCare, CVS with Aetna, Elevance with Anthem and CareMore, Cigna with HCSC/HealthSpring, Blue Cross NC with CuraCor). Ownership changes can create jumps in the series.
